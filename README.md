# Maven Fuzzy Factory — Sales Performance Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI dashboard analyzing e-commerce sales data for Maven Fuzzy Factory, an online retailer. The project covers data modeling, DAX measures, and business-driven analysis across sales, marketing, product profitability, and refunds.

## Key Insights
- **bsearch converts more efficiently than gsearch** (7.19% vs 6.75%), despite gsearch driving far more traffic — suggesting marketing spend allocation deserves review.
- **The Original Mr. Fuzzy drives the highest revenue but has the lowest profit margin** among all products, while lower-volume products like Birthday Sugar Panda are significantly more profitable per sale.
- **Profit margin has improved over time** (61% → 63.5%) alongside revenue growth, indicating increasing operational efficiency.
- **Refunds total $85.34K**, roughly 7% of net profit, concentrated in the highest-selling product.

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Sales Overview** — KPIs, revenue trend, marketing channel performance
2. **Product Analysis** — profitability by product
3. **Marketing & Growth** — conversion rate, AOV, profit margin trend
4. **Refund Analysis** — refund volume and financial impact
